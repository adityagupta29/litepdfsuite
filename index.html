Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to your servers?

No. Lite PDF Suite runs entirely on your device using JavaScript. Your files never leave your browser, which guarantees 100% privacy and data security.

Is there a file size limit?

While there is no hard limit set by us, the tool is optimized to handle PDF files up to 500MB smoothly. Performance may vary based on your computer's RAM.

Do I need to install any software?

No installation required. Lite PDF Suite works natively in modern web browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

Will the final PDF have a watermark?

Absolutely not. We believe in providing clean, professional tools. Your output PDF will have zero watermarks or hidden metadata added by us.

Is Lite PDF Suite really free to use?

Yes. All 16 tools are free, with no sign-up, subscription, or usage caps, including for commercial and office use. There's nothing to unlock behind a paywall.

Can I use it to meet exam form photo and PDF size limits (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, PAN)?

Yes. The Photo & Signature Resizer and Compress PDF tools both support hitting an exact target file size in KB, which covers the strict upload limits set by most Indian exam and government portals.

Does Lite PDF Suite work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every tool runs in your phone's browser the same way it does on desktop, since all processing happens on-device rather than in an app you'd need to install.

Is it safe to add a password or e-sign a document here?

Yes. Protect & Unlock PDF uses the QPDF engine compiled to run locally in your browser, and e-Sign PDF never transmits your signature anywhere. Both operations happen entirely offline from your file's perspective.

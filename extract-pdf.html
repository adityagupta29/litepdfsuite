Extract Text and Images Guide

Extracting content from a PDF means pulling out the plain text or the embedded images without needing to retype anything or take manual screenshots. Lite PDF Suite's extraction tool reads through a document and lets you save the text as a plain text file, or grab every embedded image as a separate PNG, all inside your browser with nothing uploaded to a server. This is especially useful for scanned reports, research papers, or design files where you need the raw content rather than the formatted page. Because processing happens locally, even a document with dozens of embedded images extracts quickly, without waiting on an external service to finish. It also avoids the awkward process of manually screenshotting each image out of a PDF reader one at a time.

Key Features

The extraction tool separates two common needs into simple one click actions, so you only download what you actually need.

  • Text extraction: pull all readable text out of a PDF and save it as a plain text file.
  • Image extraction: grab every embedded image in the document as individual PNG files.
  • Batch image export: download every extracted image at once instead of one at a time.
  • Page by page control: choose to extract from the whole document or specific pages.
  • No quality loss: images are extracted at their original embedded resolution.
  • Clean plain text output: extracted text is free of layout artifacts, ready to paste elsewhere.

How to Use It

Pulling content out of a PDF takes just a couple of clicks, whether you need a handful of images or the entire body of text.

  1. Upload the PDF you want to extract content from.
  2. Choose whether you want text, images, or both.
  3. Let the tool scan through the document locally in your browser.
  4. Review the extracted text or image thumbnails.
  5. Click Download to save the results to your device.

Images download as a batch, so you never have to save each one individually by hand. Text can be copied directly or downloaded as a file for use elsewhere.

Common Use Cases

Extraction is useful any time you need raw content instead of a formatted page, especially when the original source files are no longer available.

  • Pulling text out of a scanned report to paste into another document.
  • Grabbing product photos or diagrams embedded inside a supplier's PDF catalog.
  • Saving charts or figures from a research paper for a presentation.
  • Recovering text from an old PDF when the original source file is lost.
  • Collecting every image from a design portfolio PDF in one download.
  • Pulling a diagram out of a manual to reuse in internal documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is extraction free to use? Yes, extracting text and images on Lite PDF Suite is completely free.

Does my file get uploaded anywhere? No, extraction happens entirely inside your browser on your own device.

What format are extracted images saved in? Every image is saved as a PNG file at its original embedded resolution.

Can I extract from only some pages instead of the whole file? Yes, you can choose specific pages if you do not need the entire document.

Does it work on scanned PDFs that are really just images? Text extraction needs a searchable PDF, so a purely scanned file may need OCR first before text can be pulled out.

Retyping text or manually saving images out of a PDF one at a time is slow and easy to get wrong, especially with a long report or a catalog full of photos. Lite PDF Suite's extraction tool removes that repetitive work entirely, pulling out everything you need in one pass so you can focus on using the content rather than collecting it. Because the tool runs locally, even a large document with many pages and images extracts quickly without any waiting on an outside service.

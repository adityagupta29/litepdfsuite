Crop PDF Guide

Cropping a PDF means trimming away part of a page, most often to remove wide scanner borders, blank margins, or unwanted content around the edges of a document. Lite PDF Suite's crop tool lets you drag a crop box directly over a live preview of the page, so you can see exactly what will be kept and what will be removed before making any change. Everything runs inside your browser, meaning the document never leaves your device during the process. You can apply the same crop across an entire document scanned at the same size, or adjust individual pages separately when a batch of scans came out slightly different from each other. This flexibility matters because real world scans are rarely perfectly consistent from page to page.

Key Features

The crop tool is built around a simple drag based selection, so there is nothing to configure beyond dragging the box you want.

  • Visual crop box: drag the edges of a box directly on the page preview.
  • Apply to all pages: use one crop setting across an entire document at once.
  • Per page adjustment: fine tune the crop on individual pages when needed.
  • Live preview: see exactly what will be removed before saving anything.
  • No quality loss: cropping trims the page without recompressing the content.
  • Undo support: reset a crop and try again if the first attempt was not right.

How to Use It

Cropping a page only takes a drag and a click, and you can preview the result before it becomes permanent.

  1. Upload the PDF you want to crop.
  2. Drag the crop box over the area you want to keep.
  3. Apply the crop to the whole document or just the current page.
  4. Check the preview to make sure nothing important was trimmed.
  5. Click Download to save the cropped PDF.

Adjustments happen live, so you can fine tune the crop box until the page looks exactly right. You can always start the crop over if the first attempt removes too much or too little.

Common Use Cases

Cropping is one of the most common fixes needed after scanning paper documents, since scanners rarely capture a page perfectly.

  • Removing wide black borders left behind by an old or poorly calibrated scanner.
  • Trimming excess white margin from a photographed page before printing.
  • Cutting a document down to just the content area for a cleaner presentation.
  • Removing a header or footer that should not appear in a shared version.
  • Preparing a scanned form to match a specific paper size requirement.
  • Trimming a scanned book page down to just the printed text block.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is cropping a PDF free? Yes, the crop tool on Lite PDF Suite is completely free to use.

Can I crop only some pages instead of the whole file? Yes, each page can be cropped individually if a batch of scans is not perfectly consistent.

Does cropping reduce the quality of the page? No, cropping only removes the selected area and does not recompress the remaining content.

Is my file uploaded anywhere during cropping? No, the entire process happens locally inside your browser.

Can I undo a crop if I remove too much? Yes, you can reset the crop box and adjust it again before downloading the final file.
